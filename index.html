MOLT
A pocket roguelike for the road. Crawl, fight, and molt into something new. Short runs. Saves anywhere. No twitchy aiming.

How to play

You are a small beast in a deep burrow. Descend room by room. Every run takes 10–15 minutes and saves after every action — close the app in a tunnel, come back later, nothing lost.

⚔️ Combat is turn-based — no aiming, no timing pressure. Each turn the enemy telegraphs its move. Read it, then tap one action:

⚔️ Strike deal damage — but you're exposed.
🛡️ Guard soak the hit — great vs big blows.
💨 Dodge gamble to avoid it entirely.

A huge red intent means guard or dodge. A calm intent means strike freely. That's the whole game — reading and choosing.

🧬 The Molt. Every few rooms you reach a molt chamber and are offered 4 new forms — keep 2. Trade claws for wings, armor for speed. This is your run's real decision. Build a beast.

Fall in battle and the run ends — but you keep how deep you got. Then you crawl again.
